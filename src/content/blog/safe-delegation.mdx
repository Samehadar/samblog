takeaways:
  - "Delegation most often runs into a lack of trust, and trust follows transparency — but that is not the only reason."
  - "Hand work off inside a safety net — processes, metrics and budgets — where a mistake gets noticed in time."
  - "Freedom is passed down in steps: approve every step → a final approval → a budget with a review after the fact. The emergency brake stays with you."
  - "Watch the system, not the tasks: a traffic-light status over several metrics and escalation rules agreed in advance."
  - "Your role is to organise the work, not to do it: strategy, key people, living processes and the exceptions."
