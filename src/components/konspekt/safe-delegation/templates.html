Four tables you can copy into your wiki and fill in for yourself. The fields come from sections 2, 4, 5 and 6, the examples from this article. The thresholds and deadlines in them are a guide, not a norm: pick your own.

1. Process card (section 2)

Fill it in before handing work off through a process. If there's nothing to put in some field, the process isn't ready to be handed off yet.

Action rules worth writing next to the table: Warn means the project manager notifies and fixes by next week; a second Warn in a row escalates to the CTO; a third becomes Critical, with an action plan and the leadership team brought in; Uncertain means sort it out before the next snapshot.
