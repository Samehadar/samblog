Oversight
Constant attention to the system (processes, metrics, budgets) instead of controlling individual tasks.
Safe-delegation infrastructure
An environment where a mistake by whoever does the work is noticed in time and doesn't become fatal: oversight + processes + metrics + budgets.
Emergency brake
The leader's right to stop a handed-off process at any moment.
Business process, owner, customer
Recurring actions that lead to a result; the one accountable for the result; the one who needs the result.
RACI
Responsibility assignment per step: Responsible — does the work, Accountable — answers for it, Consulted — gives input, Informed — is kept up to date.
Cross-pollination
Training process participants through experienced colleagues.
Threshold
A metric's cut-off points: what's normal, what needs attention, what's a crisis.
Gaming a metric
Improving one metric at the expense of other dimensions of the project.
Noise
A metric that stays red with no real problem behind it.
Project status
The summary traffic light of project health: Good / Warn / Critical / Uncertain.
Bug leakage
The share of bugs that reached production.
Lead time, cycle time
Time from a task appearing to release; from the start of work on it to done.
WIP
Work in progress: how many tasks are in progress at once.
SLO, error budget
The target reliability level of a service and the allowed amount of failure; budget used up — releases slow down.
DACI
Roles in a decision: Driver — drives, Approver — approves, Contributors — advise, Informed — are kept up to date.
Team morale score, People Status
The average score of a monthly team survey (1–5); a subjective assessment of an employee's loyalty and satisfaction that the manager or HR sets after a one-on-one.
CSAT, NPS
Client satisfaction; the client's willingness to recommend the company.
Budget, operational budget
A plan bounded in time; a limit on an activity in hours or units.
Buffer
The leader's reserve for overruns and emergencies.
Payroll, pay structure
Total payroll cost; the list of positions with headcount and salary bands per level.
Financial model
The model of the company's income and expenses into which all budgets roll up.
OKR
Objective — a qualitative goal; Key Results — measurable results by which its achievement is judged.
Utilisation
The share of employees' billable (project) time.
